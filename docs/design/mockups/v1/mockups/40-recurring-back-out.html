Why no "undo"? Because "undo" would mean cancelling 9 appointments the patient already committed to. The system doesn't decide that for you — you do, one at a time, with an audit trail and a reason you write. That's BR-052 (per-occurrence independence), BRD §4.6 (never silently invalidate), and PRD §18 (audit every change). Three rules, one principle: act, don't un-act.
