ConflictModal — universal pattern

A three-part shape: what happened · why · what you can do. Used by F-01, F-02, F-05, F-06, F-07, F-13, F-16. Never a dead end — every modal offers a path forward.

F-01 · Patient booking

Why: Availability is the server's source of truth. Your hold expired before checkout completed.
rule: BR-024 · atomic_slot_hold_timeout
F-01 · Patient booking
!

You already have an appointment with Dr. Al-Rashid

An appointment is already scheduled for Thu, Aug 21 · 14:45 with Dr. Al-Rashid at Riyadh Olaya.
Why: One future appointment per doctor is the rule (BR-007). Booking another would silently double-book your time with the same clinician.
rule: BR-007 · one_future_appt_per_doctor
F-05 · Patient reschedule
!

You have another appointment at this time

Moving to Sat, Aug 23 · 11:15 conflicts with your existing appointment with Dr. Sara at Riyadh Malqa.
Why: Patient cannot be in two places at once. The slot finder hides overlapping options, but the rule fires when you confirm anyway.
rule: BR-006 · patient_overlap_check
F-17 · Admin branches
⊘

Branch has 14 future appointments

Deactivating Riyadh Olaya would leave 14 future appointments orphaned. The system cannot close a clinic that is currently serving patients.
Why: Deactivation requires zero future appointments (BR-059). Patients keep their bookings; no new bookings can be made.
rule: BR-059 · deactivate_requires_zero_future_appts
