7.5 Field

Inputs, selects, textareas. Required marker is the red asterisk, never "(required)".

We send a 6-digit code here to verify.

7.6 Modal — ConflictModal

Three slots: what happened · why · what you can do. Never block with a dead-end OK.

⚠

That slot was just booked.

Dr. Al-Rashid's Saturday 10:00 was claimed 12 seconds ago.

Pick another time with Dr. Al-Rashid on Saturday, or try the same slot tomorrow.

Rule BR-005: each slot is held by exactly one appointment. No double-booking.

7.7 Toast

Top-right, auto-dismiss 3s. Tone variants: success, info, warning, error.

ⓘSchedule saved.
⚠2 appointments are outside branch hours.
✕Slot was just booked. Pick another.
