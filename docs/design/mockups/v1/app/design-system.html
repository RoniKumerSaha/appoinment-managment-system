§7.4Section (numbered step)

Stacked-vertical flow primitive. Three step states.

2
Future step
Outlined — light fill, brand text. Reached later in the flow.
3
Current step
Filled brand-primary + success halo. Always the user's focus.
1
Completed
Filled, no halo. Already in the past — focus stays on current.
2
Cancel · current-danger
Destructive flow only — danger ring signals what you're doing.
