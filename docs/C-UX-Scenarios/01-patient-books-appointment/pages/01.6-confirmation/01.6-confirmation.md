# Page 01.6 — Confirmation (The Receipt)

**Scenario:** 01 — Patient Books an Appointment
**Persona:** Maya (Patient)
**Position in flow:** Exit — appointment is Scheduled

---

## Purpose

Show the patient that her appointment is real, give her a screenshot-able receipt, and point her to the place she can return to (the OTP-gated dashboard).

This page is the *product*. Everything before it earned the right to show it.

## Exit action

Tap "View my appointments" → `01.7-otp-verification/` → `01.8-patient-dashboard/`.

## Components

| Component | Notes |
|---|---|
| Confirmation banner | Big checkmark + "You're booked." — a moment of relief. |
| Receipt card | Doctor + specialty, date, time, branch + address, type, booking ref. Self-contained. Screenshot-friendly. |
| Phone confirmation hint | "We sent a code to +20 ███ ███ 4567. Use it any time to view or cancel." — primes her for the OTP gate without forcing it now. |
| Primary CTA | "View my appointments" — takes her to the OTP gate. |
| Secondary actions | "Add to calendar" (ICS download), "Take a screenshot" (instructional hint, not a button that screenshots — that's browser-blocked). |
| Phone-call fallback | "Need to change something? Call the clinic." — for the NG-P3 edge case. Real phone number. |

## Behavioral notes

- **This page is the receipt.** The visual hierarchy is: doctor/time/branch first, everything else secondary. Screenshot-able. Printable.
- **The booking ref is shown.** A short, human-readable reference ("A-2026-08-19-1530") for support calls. Not a UUID.
- **The phone number is partially masked.** "+20 ███ ███ 4567" — she sees what number she used without exposing it to over-the-shoulder viewers.
- **The phone-call fallback is intentional.** NG-P3 says she fears having to call to verify. We're not calling her to verify; we're offering a phone number for when the OTP path fails.
- **"Add to calendar"** is an ICS download — a file she can open in her phone's calendar. Server-side generated. Not a third-party calendar integration.
- **No marketing.** No "follow us on social." No "subscribe to our newsletter." This page has one job.

## Empty / error states

This page is only reached on success. There is no error state. If the user lands here by URL manipulation, redirect to doctor list.

## Accessibility

- Booking ref is selectable text.
- Calendar download button is keyboard-accessible.
- Color contrast meets WCAG AA on the receipt card.

## Mobile vs desktop

Mobile: full-width receipt card. Desktop: max-width 520px centered, with the receipt card prominently displayed.

## Why this page matters

This is the first time the patient has ever had a *receipt* for an appointment at this clinic. In the before-state (Product Brief §3), she had a feeling. In the after-state, she has a card she can come back to. Every UX choice on this page defends that shift.
