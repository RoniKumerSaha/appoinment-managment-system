# Page 01.8 — Patient Dashboard

**Scenario:** 01 — Patient Books an Appointment (returning visit)
**Persona:** Maya (Patient)
**Position in flow:** Post-OTP dashboard

---

## Purpose

Show the patient her upcoming appointments. Give her a path to cancel or reschedule each one. This is the *visibility* half of the product promise.

## Exit action

Tap appointment → reschedule (Phase 4 design). Tap "Book another" → `01.1-doctor-list/`.

## Components

| Component | Notes |
|---|---|
| Header | "Your appointments" + masked phone + sign out. |
| Upcoming list | Sorted ascending by date. |
| Appointment card | Date, time, doctor, branch, type, action buttons. |
| Sign out | Clears OTP session. |
| "Book another" | Returns to doctor list. |

## Behavioral notes

- **Only upcoming, non-cancelled appointments are shown** (FR-032: cancelled appointments shall not appear in the patient-facing upcoming list).
- **No past appointments.** PRD §2.2 explicitly excludes patient-facing historical appointment list.
- **Sort ascending by date** so the next appointment is on top.
- **Reschedule and Cancel are equal-weight actions.** Both available from the card. (Reschedule goes to a phase-4 flow that reuses slot picker, contact-info, and atomic-reservation patterns.)
- **No notifications.** SMS reminders are out of MVP scope (BRD §2.2). The patient is responsible for remembering; the dashboard is the place she checks.
- **Sign out is prominent** but not aggressive. A returning patient who doesn't sign out will see her appointments on the next visit (within session), but session expiry clears them.

## Empty / error states

| State | UI |
|---|---|
| No upcoming appointments | "You have no upcoming appointments. [Book one →]" |
| Session expired | "Your session ended. [Verify your phone again →]" |

## Accessibility

- Appointment cards are list items; each card is a region.
- Action buttons are real `<button>` elements with clear labels.

## Mobile vs desktop

Mobile: full-width cards, sticky "Book another" at bottom. Desktop: max-width 640px centered.

## Why this page matters

This is the *visibility* half of the product promise. Every time the patient opens this dashboard and sees her appointment is still there, still at the same time, still at the same branch — the trust contract is renewed. The dashboard is not a feature; it's the product.
